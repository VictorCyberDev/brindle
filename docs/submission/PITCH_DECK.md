# Pitch deck outline (10 slides)

1. **Title**: Brindle Recovery. Truth changed. Recover precisely.
2. **Problem**: agents act on facts that later change; today a person reads logs and guesses.
3. **Why now**: agents are getting authority to approve, pay and notify.
4. **Insight**: neither full rollback nor doing nothing is right. Precision is the product.
5. **How it works**: trace, decide (counterfactual materiality), recover (registry only), prove (fresh verification).
6. **Demo**: Atlas then Cedarline, with the Recovery Proof.
7. **Trust model**: Shadow, Approval, Bounded Autonomous. Irreversible effects are escalated, never rolled back.
8. **Recovery Memory**: verified recoveries advise later plans. Advisory only.
9. **Evidence**: eight-scenario evaluation and ablation (see `docs/M6_ABLATION.md`): with each safeguard removed, X goes wrong.
10. **Status and ask**: working engine and private beta; no connectors to customer systems yet, no customers, no certifications. Ask: design partners for Approval Mode.

Appendix: security review, API, roadmap.
